Albanese Lab Tools

Plot raw instrument exports in your browser and save publication-ready figures as PNG or SVG. Each tool overlays several samples per panel and shows the processed numbers in a data table.

Spectroscopy and calorimetry

CD Spectra Plotter

Mean residue ellipticity spectra (190 to 260 nm) and thermal melts, with optional buffer blank subtraction.

Input: Jasco .txt export

ITC Thermogram Plotter

Raw injection-heat traces (DP versus time) overlaid by sample, with an optional temperature label.

Input: 2-column raw data .csv

Chromatography

His-Tag Purification Plotter

Normalized Ni-NTA / IMAC affinity purification traces.

Input: Unicorn .asc export

SEC Trace Plotter

Preparative Superdex 75 and 200 traces, with predicted monomer and dimer elution windows from a calibration curve.

Input: Unicorn .asc + calibration .csv

Analytical SEC Plotter

Analytical aSEC 75 and 200 traces, with predicted monomer and dimer elution windows from a calibration curve.

Input: Unicorn .asc + calibration .csv

Biotage Flash Plotter

Isolera flash runs: normalized UV with the %B gradient on a second axis. Includes demo data to try it out.

Input: Isolera .csv export

Literature

Lit Feed

New papers and graphical abstracts from ACS, Nature, Science, Cell Press, RSC, Wiley, PNAS, eLife and preprint servers, updated every morning.

Your data stays on your computer. Files are read by your browser and never uploaded. Nothing is saved between visits, so save your figures before closing the page. Exported figures always use a white background and light colors, even if your computer is in dark mode.
